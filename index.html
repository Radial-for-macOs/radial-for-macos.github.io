Step 2: Select

Choose from context-aware actions, sub-menus, or community shortcuts. Minimal movement required.

Step 3: Execute

Your workflow runs instantly—apps open, text inserts, files process. Multi-step tasks complete in one gesture.

No. Drag-and-drop actions suffice. AppleScript and shell scripts available for power users.

Hotkeys, hot corners, mouse buttons, cursor shake. Fully configurable.

Does it work everywhere?

Yes, across all macOS applications.
